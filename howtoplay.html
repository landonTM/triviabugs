THE BASICS

  1. Start a game with /trivia start (or join one already running). Bertha posts a question.
  2. Type your answer straight into the chat. No command needed, and you can guess as often as you like.
  3. The first correct answer wins the round. You have 60 seconds, with a hint at 20s and another at 40s.
  4. Spelling is forgiving: one typo is fine on answers of 4+ letters, and accents and capitals don't matter.
  5. Every 10th round is an insane round: name as many answers to one question as you can.
  6. Most points at the end wins. Everyone who played earns XP toward their level.

A game ends early if nobody types anything in the channel for 3 minutes 30 seconds.

STREAKS

  • Win rounds back to back to build a streak. It carries over into your next game in the same channel.
  • It ends when someone else wins a round, or a round goes by with nobody getting it.
  • A longer streak means more XP at the end of the game (see XP).

DUELS

HOW A DUEL WORKS

  • /duel challenge someone and pick a length: Best of 3, 5, 7, 9 or 10, or First to 3, 5, 7 or 10.
  • Before it starts, each of you chooses whether to ban categories (2 or 3 each, taking turns).
  • 30 seconds per question, with one hint at 15 seconds. First correct answer takes the point.
  • Best of: it ends as soon as the leader can't be caught. A tie at the end goes to a sudden-death insane-round tiebreaker.
  • First to: no question limit. If you both go quiet for 5 questions in a row, it ends as a tie.
  • The winner gets 1,500 XP; the loser gets 150 XP for every question they won. Hit Rematch to go again.
  • Every season has a duel rating (ELO). Longer duels move it more, and the top 3 at the end of a season win XP.

XP & LEVELS

There are 100 levels. Each level unlocks rewards: cosmetics, and XP Boosts at levels 15, 30, 45, 60, 75 and 90. Check yours with /rank.

WHAT EARNS MORE XP IN A GAME

  • Longer games: more rounds, more XP.
  • Correct answers: the biggest factor, measured against whoever answered the most that game.
  • Speed: fast answers are worth up to 10% more, slow ones up to 10% less.
  • Your best streak: +2% for every answer in your best streak that game, with no cap.
  • More players: 4 players is normal; fewer earns less, and 5, 6 or 7+ earn more.
  • More categories: mixing categories is worth up to 10% more.

BONUSES

  • Replay bonus: join another game within 10 minutes of your last one ending for +5% XP, up to +25% (games of 5+ rounds).
  • Double XP weekends: every Saturday and Sunday (UTC), all game XP is doubled.
  • Daily Trivia is always 2×, and hardcore is 1.5×.
  • XP Boosts (/boost): 2× XP just for you. They only use up time while you're actually in a game, and you can pause them.
  • Achievements and missions pay XP too. Check yours with /achievements.

SEASONS

  • A season runs for several weeks, Monday to Monday. Season 0 ends Monday, November 2 at 10 AM Eastern, when Season 1 begins.
  • Games and duels fill your season pass (/pass), which unlocks seasonal cosmetics tier by tier. Double XP and boosts don't speed it up; hardcore's 1.5× does.
  • The top 3 in points over the season win 50,000 / 25,000 / 10,000 XP. The top 3 duel ratings win 20,000 / 10,000 / 5,000.
  • Each season has 3 Spotlight categories worth 1.5× points.
  • Season 0 is solo. Season 1's format (Duos, Trios, Squads, 6v6 or Round Robin) is drawn on November 2. See /team view.
